eInvoicing in the UAE: what changes for businesses in 2026–2027

Author: Nana Zhorzholiani, Accountant at EFIX GRP12 min read

The UAE has already started moving to a mandatory electronic invoicing system — eInvoicing. This is not simply emailing a PDF invoice. An electronic invoice must be created in a structured format, delivered to the buyer through an accredited provider and, at the same time, reported to the tax system.

The pilot phase and voluntary adoption started on 1 July 2026. For companies with annual revenue equal to or above AED 50 million, mandatory application starts on 1 January 2027. For companies with annual revenue below AED 50 million, it starts on 1 July 2027. That makes 2026 the year to check your accounting system, customer and supplier data, invoicing processes and readiness to integrate with an Accredited Service Provider (ASP).

In short: what you need to know

eInvoicing in the UAE applies to B2B and B2G transactions and does not depend on VAT registration status alone. A PDF, Word file, scan or a regular invoice sent by email is not an eInvoice. Under the mandatory regime, a company must work through an Accredited Service Provider (ASP).

    What is eInvoicing in the UAE

    An electronic invoice (eInvoice) is an invoice that is issued, transmitted and received in a structured electronic format that allows the data to be processed automatically. In the UAE, the exchange is built on the Peppol infrastructure and the PINT-AE specification.

    In other words, the familiar PDF remains a convenient visual document for people, but on its own it is not an electronic invoice under the new system. What matters is the structured data set that can be read by the accounting system, the provider and the Federal Tax Authority without manual entry.

    How the system works: the five-corner model

    The current UAE eInvoicing model has five participants. The supplier creates the invoice data in its accounting system, the supplier's Accredited Service Provider (ASP) validates the document and sends it via Peppol, the buyer's ASP delivers it to the buyer's system, and the relevant data is reported to the UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) at the same time.

    Who is subject to mandatory eInvoicing

    The system applies to anyone doing business in the UAE, for business transactions within its scope. The obligation is not limited to VAT-registered companies: not having a VAT TRN does not, by itself, take a business out of the system.

    B2B

    Transactions between businesses.

    B2G

    Goods and services supplied by businesses to government entities.

    G2B and G2G

    The relevant transactions of government entities.

    Free Zone

    Free zone companies do not get an automatic exemption just because they are registered in a free zone.

    Not VAT-registered

    Companies without VAT registration can fall within the system if they carry out the relevant business transactions.

    Which transactions are not yet covered by the mandate

    B2C transactions — sales to end consumers who are not conducting business — are not subject to mandatory eInvoicing at this stage. A company that deals exclusively in B2C does not have to join until a separate decision brings such transactions into scope.

    There are also specific exclusions, including for:

    • certain sovereign activities of government entities;
    • certain international passenger air transport and related services;
    • international air cargo transport under an air waybill (a temporary exclusion for a fixed period);
    • certain financial services that are VAT-exempt or zero-rated under the applicable rules.
    ImportantExclusions should be assessed transaction by transaction, not by the name of the industry. If a company has both B2C and B2B transactions, the B2B part may fall under eInvoicing.

    What is an Accredited Service Provider (ASP)

    An ASP is an accredited service provider through which a company connects to the eInvoicing system. It handles the technical exchange of electronic invoices via Peppol, data transmission, the required validations and interaction with the system.

    Not once your company falls under the mandate. A PDF, Word file, scan, image or email attachment is not an eInvoice, because it lacks the mandatory structured format required for automated data transmission and processing.

    The main challenge with eInvoicing is usually not the connection to the provider itself, but the quality of your data and internal processes. If bookkeeping is done manually, counterparty details are incomplete or invoice data is not standardised, integration can expose a large number of errors.

    What companies using Zoho Books, Xero and ERP systems need to know

    Having modern accounting software does not mean you are automatically ready for eInvoicing in the UAE. The system must be able to transmit all mandatory fields in a format supported by your chosen ASP, and your business processes must meet the PINT-AE requirements.

    In practice, there are three possible scenarios:

    Scenario 1

    Direct ERP integration with the ASP

    Scenario 2

    Connection through a ready-made connector

    Scenario 3

    Middleware that converts data from your accounting system into the required format

    The best option depends on your invoice volume, the complexity of the business and the software in use.

    Deadline for issuing an electronic invoice

    14days

    For VAT-registered companies, the deadlines set by VAT legislation apply. In other cases, an eInvoice or eCredit Note must be issued and transmitted through the system within the prescribed period, which as a general rule must not exceed 14 days from the Date of Business Transaction.

    The Date of Business Transaction is determined under the applicable rules and may depend on when the transaction takes place or when payment is received. That is why automating invoice dates and transaction recognition is an important part of the setup.

    Advance payments and credit notes

    Advance payments

    When an advance payment is received, a tax invoice must be issued at the time of payment if VAT rules require it. The final invoice then reflects the remaining amount rather than the full contract value again.

    eCredit Note

    An electronic credit note (eCredit Note) is used, in particular, when a transaction is cancelled, its value is reduced, an amount is refunded in full or in part, or an administrative or numerical error is corrected.

    What happens if a company is not ready in time

    Separate administrative penalties apply to breaches of the eInvoicing rules. They take effect once a company reaches its mandatory phase; voluntary participants are not subject to them before their mandatory date.

    eInvoicing in the UAE: frequently asked questions

    When will eInvoicing become mandatory in the UAE?
    For companies with annual revenue equal to or above AED 50 million, the mandatory go-live is 1 January 2027. For companies with annual revenue below AED 50 million, it is 1 July 2027. For government entities, it is 1 October 2027.
    Do we need to take action in 2026?
    Companies with annual revenue equal to or above AED 50 million must appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) no later than 30 October 2026. This deadline was extended from 31 July 2026 by Ministerial Resolution No. 66 of 2026. In addition, any business can join voluntarily from 1 July 2026.
    Is a PDF invoice an eInvoice?
    No. A PDF, Word file, scan, image or regular email is not an eInvoice. An electronic invoice must exist in a structured format suitable for automated processing and transmission.
    Does a business without VAT registration have to use eInvoicing?
    Possibly. The scope of eInvoicing is wider than VAT registration status. If a person conducts business in the UAE and carries out transactions within the scope of the system, not having a VAT TRN does not by itself exempt it from these requirements.
    Does eInvoicing apply to free zone companies?
    Yes. Registering a company in a free zone does not by itself create an exemption from eInvoicing. The type of business and the specific transactions need to be assessed.
    Do we need to issue an eInvoice to an individual?
    At this stage, B2C transactions with end consumers are not in the mandatory regime. If the buyer is acting in the course of a business, the transaction may already count as B2B.
    What is an ASP?
    An ASP (Accredited Service Provider) is an accredited provider through which a company sends and receives electronic invoices within the UAE eInvoicing system.
    Can we choose any IT provider?
    Mandatory eInvoicing requires a service provider accredited by the UAE Ministry of Finance. When choosing one, it is also important to check compatibility with your accounting system and business processes.
    Do we need to replace Zoho Books or other accounting software?
    Not necessarily. It depends on whether your current system supports the required integration directly or through a connector / ASP. A technical and accounting assessment should come first.
    Are there penalties for non-compliance?
    Yes. Once your mandatory date arrives, there are specific penalties for failing to implement the system, for late transmission of eInvoices and eCredit Notes, and for certain breaches of notification obligations.

    Key takeaways for businesses

    eInvoicing in the UAE is a shift from a conventional document to the automated exchange of structured tax and commercial data. For most companies, the mandatory regime starts in 2027, but preparation should not be left to the last month: data quality, choosing an ASP and integrating with your accounting system all take time.

    Companies that put their master data and invoicing processes in order early will be able to move to the new system far more smoothly and reduce the risk of technical errors, rejected documents and penalties after go-live.
